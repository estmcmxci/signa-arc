Security & boundaries · Arc Testnet

Know what the control proves.

Signa Covenant enforces a draw condition against signed evidence. The condition is only as meaningful as the policy and the assertions it receives.

Demonstration only. Fictional exposure, mock issuer feeds and testnet USDC. No bank or hedge venue is integrated. No production deployment is represented.

What is enforced

The facility policy sets a minimum coverage ratio, freshness and maturity limits, a haircut and retained reserve. The coverage engine evaluates credential eligibility. The vault checks the covenant within the draw transaction and enforces its reserve independently.

Two distinct approved issuer keys attest exposure and hedge state. Distinct keys do not prove that two legally independent institutions stand behind them. The testnet feeds are explicitly labelled mocks.

What a signature does not prove

A signature authenticates who asserted a value and binds that assertion to its signed fields. It does not establish that a hedge legally exists, that its terms are enforceable, that a counterparty will perform or that the underlying evidence is accurate.

Signa does not price, recommend, arrange or execute derivatives. This demonstration does not originate loans or provide financial advice.

Exceptions are governed and recorded

The deployed facility admin is a Privy wallet controlled by a 2-of-2 key quorum. The recorded waiver required a risk officer and treasury lead. A waiver permits an exception for a limited period; it does not make the underlying coverage compliant.

The admin address is fixed for this facility. Contract rules and pre-validation bound waiver duration. Privy signs the transaction; a separate broadcast sends it to Arc.

The approver console is a local demonstration server without authentication. Its approver keys use browser WebCrypto, not passkeys. It is not a production approval service.

Operational limits

  • The operator desk reads one deployed facility. Contract reads can become unavailable or stale when its RPC connection is interrupted.
  • A browser simulation describes a specific account, request and chain state. The transaction must be simulated again before signing; later state can still change its outcome.
  • A mined revert consumes gas. A missing receipt is not proof that a transaction was dropped or reverted.
  • Historical receipts, locally recorded decisions and current state have different scopes. The desk is not a complete independent audit log.
  • No public uptime monitor or service-level commitment is provided for this demonstration.
