01 / The problem

Capital moves on demand.
Reconciliation runs on a calendar.

Loan balances live in a servicing system. Hedges live at a bank or broker. Available capital lives somewhere else. Teams reconcile those records, while draw requests keep arriving.

The missing step is a coverage check at the moment capital moves.

02 / The mechanism

One rule, from evidence to release.

The demonstration joins signed assertions to a USDC credit vault on Arc. The hedge remains offchain; the draw condition is enforced onchain.

  1. 01

    Freeze the policy

    Set the currency pair, coverage minimum, freshness window and retained reserve. Frozen terms give every decision the same reference.

    Facility Registry
  2. 02

    Authenticate both sides

    Approved, distinct issuer keys sign exposure and hedge credentials. Mock issuers in this demonstration.

    Credential Registry
  3. 03

    Count only eligible coverage

    Check admissibility, apply the haircut and cap counted cover at exposure. A signed credential can still be excluded.

    Coverage Engine
  4. 04

    Make the vault obey

    Evaluate the covenant within the draw transaction. Release funds only when the draw gate and retained reserve allow it.

    Covenant Vault

03 / What it enforces

The rule travels with the money.

Coverage checked during the draw

The contract evaluates the evidence in the same transaction that releases funds. A previous green screen is no substitute for that check.

Signed does not mean eligible

Freshness, issuer approval, pair and maturity checks determine what counts. The engine reports the reason for exclusion.

Distinct issuers, explicit limits

Exposure and hedge require different approved issuer keys. This enforces key separation, not legal independence between firms.

An exception leaves a record

A waiver is dated and attributable. The recorded admin waiver required both the risk officer and treasury lead to approve.

Settlement and network fees use USDC on Arc Testnet. The application does not price, recommend or execute a derivative.

04 / The recorded proof

The same draw.
Three decisions.

The request stayed the same. The evidence changed. The covenant permitted a draw, held it when counted coverage fell, then permitted it after restoration.
